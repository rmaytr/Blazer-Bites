Why it's needed

Lunch at UAB costs too much and takes too long

Campus Dining is run by Sodexo. Students pay about $11 a meal for mostly national chains, clustered in a few buildings and largely closed by Friday afternoon.

~$11per meal swipe on the Gold Block plans
3 buildingshold almost all campus food, so noon lines eat a short class gap
5 p.m. Fridaymost retail closes, and several spots are shut all weekend
EVERYDAYthe official app shows menus only, and students say almost no one uses it

Time, price and taste are hypotheses we will confirm with a 300-student survey before buying equipment.

Taste comes first.

A chef owns the plate

A professional chef mentor tastes the food before every service, the same final check Jefferson State's student restaurant uses. Every dish has a written recipe card with weights and temperatures, and sauces, spice blends and pickles are made from scratch.

The vision

From one tent to a campus dining app

We spend almost nothing until students prove they will buy. The app follows the same rule: prove it on our own food, then offer it to the whole campus.

Spring 2027

Pilot

Launch for about $4,800: four tent pop-ups at permitted events plus 16 days on a licensed truck rented on its owner's off days. Volunteer crew, an hourly kitchen and free pre-order tools. We count every customer, wait and rating.

Fall 2027

Launch

A rented truck 3 days a week for the semester. UAB computer science and design students build our own app for credit, using pilot order data.

2028 on

Scale

Our own cart only once savings, a prize or a sponsor pay for it, never a loan. The app grows to cover the dining hall and retail, offered to Campus Dining as an upgrade to EVERYDAY.

~80orders a truck day to break even

Pays for itself in one semester. Four tent pop-ups and 16 rented-truck days bring in about $15,400 and leave about $6,200 after food, packaging, card fees and truck rental, more than the $4,800 launch. Semester two, a rented truck 3 days a week, leaves about $3,500 more. Try the profit dial →

Why about 80 orders. Each order keeps about $3.92 after food, packaging and card fees. A rented-truck day costs about $308 in rental, kitchen hours and one paid lead, so it breaks even near 80 orders. We plan for 100, in line with the 100 to 150 a day The Box at Dartmouth reached.
